# DustLab kit protocol

## Purpose

A low-cost classroom protocol for comparing dust deposition and surface output under controlled conditions. This is a screening experiment, not a Mars simulation.

## Materials (target under US$30; local prices vary)

Small PV cell, multimeter, fixed lamp, ruler, balance (borrowed), timer, sample cups, transparent box, gloves, mask, labels, graph paper and nonconductive brush. Avoid inhaling dust. Work with teacher approval and keep samples sealed. Do not use high-voltage equipment in a school replication. A low-voltage, current-limited demonstration may show field-driven motion, but it is not equivalent to the reported prototype.

## Protocol

1. Label source and replicate; record room temperature and RH.
2. Sieve comparison samples to the same size fraction. Set fixed areal loading (g/m²).
3. Fix lamp distance, angle, panel orientation and warm-up time.
4. Compare clean, uncharged dust, friction-charged dust and inert-dust control at matched loading.
5. Record voltage and short-circuit current separately; do not call voltage “power.”
6. Repeat at least 10 times per condition and report individual values, mean, SD and exclusions.
7. For removal, report initial/residual mass with balance resolution, gap, polarity, voltage, time and supervision.

## CSV template

`date,site,sample_id,condition,replicate,particle_fraction_um,loading_g_m2,rh_percent,temp_C,lamp_distance_cm,panel_angle_deg,voltage_V,current_A,power_W,initial_dust_g,residual_dust_g,gap_cm,polarity,notes`

## Contributor form specification

Collect site name, coordinates (with consent), collection date, size fraction/method, environmental conditions, experiment protocol, individual raw readings, instrument details, source/permission note, optional contact and a public-attribution checkbox. Do not collect student face images, voice or identifying details by default.
