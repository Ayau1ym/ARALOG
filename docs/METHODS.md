# Methods

## Study material

Three samples were collected 2026-07-28, 15 g each, then air-dried for one day at 21 °C and 50% RH. Sites: N1 45.398333 N, 59.795556 E; N2 45.633333 N, 60.250021 E; N3 44.828889 N, 65.519722 E.

## Experiments as reported

**Extract chemistry.** 20 g sample plus 400 mL distilled water, stirred 10 min, filtered; photometric Fe/Cu/Mn results are reported in mg/dm³. Analytical repeat count is not separately reported. Values describe aqueous extract, not bulk mineral concentrations.

**Electrical response.** S.P.A.R.K. logger, two metal electrodes 10 mm apart, 8 g sample, 10 Hz over 5 s. Aralkum samples were reported to have higher response than control. Screenshot traces near 0.285 V and 0.245 V are not assigned to samples. This is qualitative-comparative, not absolute charge.

**PV voltage.** Miniature PV cell horizontal; flashlight at 19 cm; 5 g dust spread evenly; friction charging with plastic ruler for 30 s; 22 °C and 50% RH; three repeats per condition. Reported voltage is 0.30 V clean to 0.22 V with charged Aralkum dust. Derived change: −0.08 V; relative change = (0.22−0.30)/0.30×100 = −26.7%. It is not a power/efficiency result and optical shading is not separated from electrostatic effects. SD is not reported.

**Electrostatic prototype.** Two parallel plates 2 cm apart in a transparent box, 5 g Aralkum dust, teacher-supervised ~220 kV source for 60 s. Particle movement and electrode attraction were observed. The paper reports eta=(m0−m1)/m0=25.6%; run count, actual masses and polarity are unknown. Treat it as a preliminary single-prototype report.

## NASA POWER processing

Monthly point API, 2025, parameters ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, T2M, RH2M and WS10M at the three supplied coordinates. Snapshot JSON preserves returned API values and records retrieval date and exact URL. The site charts returned monthly values. No interpolation, ranking or combined environmental index is applied.
