02 / ANALOGY BREAKER

Ask a narrower question

Properties are compared separately. Unknown values stay unknown; no global similarity percentage is computed.

Where the analogy breaks

Earth lab air: ~1013 hPa, 50% RH, 1 g. Mars: ~6 hPa CO₂, very dry, 0.38 g, different UV exposure and perchlorate context. Quartz-rich Aralkum material is not basaltic MGS-1; grain size and shape/weathering were not measured in this study.

04 / MISSION USE

A screening experiment, not a Mars replica

Use Aralkum to screen dust adhesion, charge response and cleaning concepts under a reproducible Earth setup. A result that survives controlled particle size, loading, humidity and charge controls would justify a later simulant or chamber test.

What would falsify this?

Matched-size, matched-load tests show no repeatable Aralkum/control difference; voltage loss disappears when charge is controlled; or measured removal fails to reproduce under a mass-based protocol.

Next experiments

Sieve <63 μm; fixed g/m²; n≥10 and logged RH; charged/uncharged and inert-dust controls; measure current and power; mass-based removal across gap/polarity; salt doping; XRF/XRD; extract EC/pH; basalt/MGS-1 reference; rough-vacuum CO₂ chamber.

05 / EVIDENCE & LIMITS

What supports each statement

Every claim is marked by source type. NASA data, published reference values and student measurements are kept distinct.

● MEASURED · OURS Sample sites; extract chemistry; qualitative electrical response; panel output voltage; prototype movement/cleaning report.

■ PUBLISHED NASA POWER environment snapshots; InSight energy endpoints; MGS-1 reference specifications; Mars context.

◆ HYPOTHESIS Salts or grain properties could affect charging and adhesion; panel cleaning behavior could translate to hardware screening.

○ NOT TESTED Bulk mineralogy, particle size distribution, Mars-like atmosphere, lunar performance, operational cleaning efficiency.

Study limits (verbatim)

  • n=3
  • chemistry covers only Fe/Cu/Mn
  • particle size not measured
  • tests run in Earth air, not CO₂ / low pressure / UV
