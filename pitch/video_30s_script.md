# 30-second video script

Text cards only; no author voice or face.

NASA's InSight arrays produced about 5,000 watt-hours per sol after landing and about 500 by spring 2022 as dust settled. Could Aralkum dust help screen how dust sticks to solar hardware?

ARALOG combines three local samples, preliminary bench results and NASA POWER environmental snapshots. Choose an engineering question and inspect each property with its evidence label.

The result is conditional: useful for a controlled Earth-air screening test, not a Mars replica. Particle size, low pressure and UV still need testing.

End card: **Analog fitness depends on the question being asked.** Independent project, not NASA-affiliated.
